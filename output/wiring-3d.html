Raspberry Pi — complete 3D wiring

One Pi, LCD, five-button keypad, ADC, amplifier and speaker connected together. Pi pin numbers are physical header positions.

Add two 10 kΩ resistors: keypad OUT → R1 → junction → ADC A0; junction → R2 → ADC GND. The keypad uses 5 V; the ADC uses 3.3 V. Both speaker wires go to the amplifier output terminals.

The green PAM8403 knob board is an alternative amplifier and is left disconnected here. The photographed ADC says ADS1115/ADS1015; confirm its chip for the software driver. ADC and amplifier terminal layouts are schematic because their front-side labels are not visible in the photos: match the actual printed labels. The Pi and its header are enlarged for readability. Each cable number matches the connection table.

LCD software: set BL to BCM GPIO24 (physical pin 18); BCM GPIO18 (physical pin 12) is used for audio. MAX98357A GAIN and SD are left open only with the breakout's default SD pull-up present. Solder headers or wires to bare pads. Connect Pi micro-USB power last.
