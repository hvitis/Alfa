THE RASPBERRY PI APP

HOLD TO DRAW

Screen on the left. Buttons on the right.

The alphabet lesson from the Pi. Tap the bottom button to hear the letter. Hold for 0.6 seconds to draw. Release to pause.

Spoken recordings stay on the Pi. These buttons run the same lesson.

  • Left and right change the letter and play its sound.
  • Top plays listen and brings the card in from above.
  • The button under those plays say-it-with-me, waits, then a word of praise.
  • Tap the bottom button to replay. Hold for 0.6 seconds to start or resume drawing.

Use left/right arrows to change letters. Space or Enter replays on a tap and draws on a hold.
