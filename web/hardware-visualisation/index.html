      YOUR BOARD · BUT1 / BUT2

      ADC pins & connections

      Label side up, header on the right: V, G, SCL, SDA, ADDR, ALERT, A0, A1, A2, A3. The black spacer and long pins are on the component side. Board enlarged for clarity.

      Printed pinConnection

      V means VDD; G means GND. A0 connects to the R1/R2 join. ADDR connects to G. ALERT and A1–A3 stay unwired.

      G is a shared connection: the marked ground join connects controller ground, R2 and ADDR. One yellow lead runs from that join to G. Wire colors identify connections in this diagram.

      YOUR BOARD · HEADER ORDER VERIFIED IN MAX4

      Amplifier assembly & wiring

      Component side up. Green speaker block at the top; yellow seven-pin header at the bottom. Board enlarged for clarity.

      Header labelPi 3 connection

      Seven solder joints, five jumper wires. Solder all seven header pins. Leave GAIN and SD without external wires.

      Green screw terminal

      Loosen each screw, insert the stripped speaker wire into its wire entry opening, then tighten. Neither speaker wire connects to GND.

      Disconnect Pi power before soldering or moving wires. The long header pins face upward; solder the short legs on the back.

      TWO RESISTORS · BOTH 10 kΩ

      One shared join. Three connections.

      Join these three ends together: R1 leg B, R2 leg A, and the ADC A0 wire.

      Shared join

      This is one electrical connection. It is not a third component.

        Resistors have no + or −. Either end can be leg A. The labels only help you follow this drawing.

        Make the join with solder, or use one connected breadboard row. Separate breadboard rows do not connect automatically.

        R1 reduces the signal together with R2. With two equal resistors, 5 V at OUT becomes about 2.5 V at A0.
